# Remix of Ganesh Industrial Hub

Website Design & Development Prompt – Ganesh Trading Company

Create a modern, premium, professional, fully responsive corporate website for Ganesh Trading Company, a trusted industrial distributor and stockist with 71+ years of industry experience.

Use the uploaded Ganesh Trading Company logo as the primary brand identity throughout the website. The logo uses a strong gold/yellow and black visual identity.

Brand Identity

Primary Brand Colors:

Gold: #E69D02

Black: #040404

White: #FFFFFF

Light background: #F8F8F8

The overall visual style should communicate:

Industrial excellence

Safety and reliability

Engineering expertise

Premium B2B supplier

Long-standing trust

Professionalism

Quality and durability

Use the gold color strategically for buttons, highlights, icons, borders, headings, hover states, statistics, and important visual accents. Use black for strong typography and industrial sections.

Avoid making the website look overly flashy. Maintain a premium industrial corporate aesthetic with strong typography, generous spacing, clean cards, subtle shadows, high-quality industrial imagery, and professional animations.
